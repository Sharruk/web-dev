Non-Technical Events

Take a break from tech and enjoy our fun and entertaining events!

Event 1: Photography

Description: Capture the best moments of TechNova 2026. Participants must click photos on the spot based on the given theme and submit them for judging.

Rules:

  • Individual event only.
  • Participants must bring their own camera or smartphone.
  • Theme will be announced on the day of the event.
  • Maximum 5 photos can be submitted.
  • Photo editing is not allowed.

Registration Fee: Rs. 50 per participant.

Prize Information:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 2000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 1000 + Certificate

Event 2: Quiz Competition

Description: Test your general knowledge and awareness in this fast-paced quiz competition. Topics include current affairs, science, sports, and entertainment.

Rules:

  • Team size: 2 members.
  • There will be 3 rounds: Written, Buzzer, and Rapid Fire.
  • No mobile phones allowed during the quiz.
  • Judge's decision is final.
  • Questions will be in English only.

Registration Fee: Rs. 60 per team.

Prize Information:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 2500 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 1200 + Certificate

Event 3: Treasure Hunt

Description: Follow the clues, solve puzzles, and find the hidden treasure around the college campus. The team that reaches the final clue first wins!

Rules:

  • Team size: 3 to 4 members.
  • All team members must be present throughout the hunt.
  • Clues must not be shared with other teams.
  • Destroying or hiding clues will result in disqualification.
  • Duration: Approximately 1.5 hours.

Registration Fee: Rs. 100 per team.

Prize Information:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 3000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 1500 + Certificate

Event 4: Gaming Tournament

Description: Compete in popular mobile and PC games in this exciting gaming tournament. Game titles will be announced one week before the event.

Rules:

  • Individual or team participation (depends on the game).
  • Participants must bring their own devices for mobile games.
  • PCs will be provided for PC games.
  • No hacks, cheats, or mods allowed.
  • Matches will be conducted in a knock-out format.

Registration Fee: Rs. 80 per participant.

Prize Information:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 2000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 1000 + Certificate
