Welcome to TechNova 2026

Theme: Innovate. Integrate. Inspire.

TechNova 2026 is a national-level technical symposium organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Join us for an exciting day of technical and non-technical events, guest lectures, and prize distributions.
