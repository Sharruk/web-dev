Technical Events

Participate in our exciting technical competitions and showcase your talent!

Event 1: Paper Presentation

Description: Present innovative research ideas on current technology topics. Teams must submit an abstract before the event and present their paper in front of a panel of judges.

Rules:

  • Team size: 1 to 2 members.
  • Abstract submission deadline: March 10, 2026.
  • Presentation time: 10 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A.
  • Topic must be related to Computer Science or IT.
  • Plagiarism will lead to disqualification.

Prize Details:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 5000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 3000 + Certificate

Event 2: Web Design Contest

Description: Build attractive and functional websites within the given time. Teams will be given a theme on the spot and must design a complete website using HTML and CSS.

Rules:

  • Team size: 1 to 2 members.
  • Duration: 3 hours.
  • Only HTML and CSS are allowed. No frameworks.
  • Internet access will not be provided.
  • Theme will be announced at the start of the event.

Prize Details:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 5000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 3000 + Certificate

Event 3: Coding Challenge

Description: Solve programming problems within a limited time. Problems range from easy to hard and test participants on data structures, algorithms, and problem-solving skills.

Rules:

  • Individual event only.
  • Duration: 2 hours.
  • Languages allowed: C, C++, Java, Python.
  • 3 to 5 problems will be given.
  • Evaluation based on correctness and time taken.

Prize Details:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 6000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 3000 + Certificate

Event 4: Project Expo

Description: Showcase innovative projects that solve real-world problems. Teams will present their working models to a panel of judges and industry experts.

Rules:

  • Team size: 2 to 4 members.
  • Working prototype must be brought to the event.
  • Project report must be submitted in advance.
  • Presentation time: 15 minutes + 10 minutes Q&A.
  • Project must be original work.

Prize Details:

Position Prize
Winner Rs. 8000 + Certificate
Runner Up Rs. 4000 + Certificate
