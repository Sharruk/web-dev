About the Symposium

Objectives

  • To provide a platform for students to showcase their technical skills.
  • To encourage innovation and creative thinking among students.
  • To bridge the gap between academia and industry.
  • To promote healthy competition and teamwork.
  • To create networking opportunities among students from various colleges.

Vision

To become the most prestigious national-level student symposium that nurtures future technology leaders and innovators.

Mission

Our mission is to organize a well-structured, competitive, and knowledge-sharing platform where students can enhance their skills, gain exposure, and develop confidence to face real-world challenges.

Organizing Department

TechNova 2026 is organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, SSN College of Engineering (Sri Sivasubramaniya Nadar College of Engineering). The department has a strong tradition of organizing student-driven events that promote technical excellence.

Why Should You Participate?

  1. Compete with talented students from across the country.
  2. Win exciting cash prizes and certificates.
  3. Get a chance to interact with industry professionals.
  4. Enhance your resume with participation certificates.
  5. Gain exposure to new technologies and ideas.

Benefits of Participation

  • Participation certificate for all registered students.
  • Winner and runner-up certificates for event winners.
  • Cash prizes for top performers.
  • Free lunch and refreshments for all participants.
  • Networking with students and faculty from other colleges.
  • Exposure to cutting-edge technical domains.
